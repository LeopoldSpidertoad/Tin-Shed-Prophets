ABOUT

We’re The Tin Shed Prophets. Five mates, one shed, and a whole lot of blues.

Formed in 2026, we’re writing new songs and putting our own spin on the classics. No backing tracks. No click. Just five people playing from the heart.

Think smoky bars, loud guitars, and songs that stick with you.
